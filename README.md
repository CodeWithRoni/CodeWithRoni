## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hey there! I'm **Md. Amzad Hossain Roni** — a **Full-Stack Software Developer** from **Bera, Pabna, Bangladesh 🇧🇩**.

I turn ideas into **useful, well-designed software** for the **web, Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS**. **JavaScript is my primary programming language**, and my work ranges from **POS & ERP systems** for real shops and **e-commerce storefronts** to **Tauri & Electron desktop apps**, **Flutter mobile tools**, **Windows utilities**, and **everyday productivity apps** — often all from **one codebase**.
